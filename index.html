Takeout Date Check

Find which photos and videos in your Google Photos Takeout export are missing a date, and whether the date can be recovered from Google's .json files or the file name.

Browser not supported. This check needs Chrome 86+ or Edge 86+. It uses the File System Access API to read files locally. Your photos never leave your computer.

Please open this file in Microsoft Edge (built into Windows 10/11) or Google Chrome.

Point this at the folder you unzipped your Google Takeout into. It reads the metadata of every photo and video in it, plus the matching .json files Google adds beside them, and shows how many are missing a date and whether the date can be recovered.

Nothing is uploaded and nothing is changed. This check only reads file dates in your browser. Your files are not modified, no file name or other detail is sent anywhere, and the check works offline.

Unzip your Takeout download first, then choose the folder that contains Google Photos. You can also choose the top-level Takeout folder; subfolders are included.
